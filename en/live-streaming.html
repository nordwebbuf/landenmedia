Live streaming

We stream live. From start to finish.

Conferences, lectures, launches and events. We handle cameras, sound, graphics and streaming so your viewers can join wherever they are.

Book a call What's included?

Everything you need. Nothing to sort out yourself.

You focus on the content and the audience. We bring the equipment, set up, test and stream.

  • Multi-camera production

    Multiple angles, vision mixing and sound that holds up all the way.

  • Streaming where your viewers are

    To your website, YouTube, Facebook or LinkedIn, all at once.

  • On-screen graphics

How a stream works.

Live streaming is quoted per event. You get a fixed price before we start.

  1. Planning

    We go through the programme, venue, audience and where the stream will be shown. Then you get a quote.

  2. Setup and testing

    We arrive in good time, set up cameras and sound and test the whole chain before anyone is watching.

    You get the recording and, if you like, short clips to share.
