Frequently asked questions

What does it cost?

Our packages start at 495 SEK per month excl. VAT. After the first call you get a clear proposal, and everything then comes together on one invoice. See all prices.

Can you take over our existing website, domain and email?

Yes. We move your domain, DNS and email for you and plan the move so that neither email nor Google rankings are lost.

What does optimising for AI answers mean?

More and more people search through AI services like ChatGPT or Google's AI Overviews. We structure the content on your website so they can understand your business and point customers to you.

Do we need to do anything ourselves?

No, not unless you want to. Tell us what should be changed or added, and we take care of the rest.

Can you stream our event live?

Yes. We handle cameras, sound, graphics and streaming to your website or social media. Live streaming is quoted per event. Read more about live streaming.
